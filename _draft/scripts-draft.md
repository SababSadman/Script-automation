# MODE 2 — PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS

## PA Phase 1 — Performance Insight Summary

- Best retention came from business-reframe hooks — Script #7's "$664M business moment" achieved the lowest skip rate (50.7%) by making a familiar topic feel like a business consequence. This topic needs the same move: reframe merch from a product decision to a cultural one.
- Scripts with 5–6s average watch time lost viewers before the body landed — value must arrive in the first 3 seconds.
- The only PROVEN CTA (#4-A) worked because it named an exact action + used risk framing. Every other CTA that ended with a tip or soft ask generated 0 conversions.
- Advice endings never converted — Scripts #3 and #5 both ended with takeaways instead of action asks.
- B-roll that shows contrast performs better than demonstration.
- The "cultural cost" angle in this topic has no direct analytics precedent — treat as Mid-tier risk unless the hook lands hard in the first 3 seconds.
- Line worth salvaging from Script #4: "That's the hard part" structure.

---

## PA Phase 2 — Salvaged Lines

- **REUSE:** "That's the hard part" structure — *"Ordering branded merch is easy. Getting your team to actually wear it — that's the hard part."*
- **REUSE:** Business-consequence reframe from Script #7 — *"This isn't a merch decision. It's a retention decision."*
- **ADAPT:** PROVEN CTA #4-A — *"Don't gamble on consistency"* → *"Don't gamble on how your team feels about your brand."*
- **REWRITE:** Script #6 opener → *"Branded merch sounds simple. But what it signals to your team is anything but."*

**[CTA]**
"DM us your quantity and we'll show you what your team could actually be proud to wear."

---

## PA Phase 4 — Why This Script Works

- **Hook:** The main hook breaks the assumption the viewer is using to avoid the real problem — "it's not because they forgot" creates an uncomfortable recognition moment. Names the cultural cost, not just the aesthetic one.
- **Body structure:** Follows PAS with visual agitation (Creative Ads Guide #6). Pain → agitate with the real cultural cost (gym shirt, brand disappears) → shift with the solution → proof with 30 years. Salvaged "hard part" reframe is embedded in the body's pivot.
- **B-roll:** Each suggestion replaces a spoken line — the storage box visual reinforces "it goes in the drawer" without saying it twice.
- **CTA:** Built on PROVEN CTA #4-A structure — specific action verb (DM us), specific ask (your quantity), specific benefit promise (what your team could actually be proud to wear).
- **Watch in review:** Main hook line is long for a 1–3 second window. Reviewer should time the delivery — if it exceeds 4 seconds, use the alternative hook or split into two punches with a half-beat pause.
