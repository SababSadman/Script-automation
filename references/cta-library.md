---

## CTA Type Definitions

| Type | Description | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard CTA** | Demands a specific action with urgency or consequence | Warm audience, purchase-intent content, ad creatives |
| **Soft CTA** | Suggests an action without pressure or urgency | Cold audience, educational content, discovery reels |
| **Advice CTA** | Ends with a tip or takeaway — not actually an action call | Avoid for conversion goals — functions as content, not a CTA |
| **Keyword CTA** | Asks viewer to DM a specific word to trigger a response | High-conversion for service businesses, easy to track |

---

## Scoring Key

- **Action Clarity** — Does the viewer know exactly what to do? (1–5)
- **Urgency Level** — Is there a reason to act now? (1–5)
- **Specificity** — Is the ask tied to a real use case or outcome? (1–5)
- **Profile Activity** — Real outcome from analytics (0, 1, or tracked DMs/clicks)

## Key Findings

**What causes CTA failure (seen in 6 of 7 scripts):**
- Ending with a tip or advice instead of an action ask
- No action verb (say, send, DM, comment, reply, book)
- No urgency signal (deadline, consequence, limited availability)
- Vague language ("reach out", "let us help", "learn more")
- CTA arriving too late — average watch time is 5–8 seconds; most viewers leave before the CTA

**What caused the one working CTA (#4-A):**
- Specific action verb: "tell us your quantity and send us your design"
- Risk framing: "don't gamble on consistency"
- Matched exactly what the viewer already cared about after watching the script
- Short enough to land even on a partial watch

## Recommended CTA Templates

These are derived from the pattern in #4-A and proven structures from the creative ads guide.

**Template A — Direct Send (best for logo or design-first services):**
> "If you have [specific situation], send us your [asset] now. We will [specific benefit] before [deadline or consequence]."

**Template B — Keyword DM (best for volume tracking and warm audience):**
> "DM us [KEYWORD] and we will send you [specific thing] today."

**Template C — Risk Frame + Action (best for production and quality content):**
> "[Don't/Stop] [negative outcome]. [Action verb] your [specific thing] and we will [specific result]."

**Template D — Urgency + Specificity (best for seasonal or time-sensitive content):**
> "[Event/deadline] is [timeframe] away. [Action verb] your [specific thing] now so we can [specific outcome] before [consequence]."
