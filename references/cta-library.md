## Recommended CTA Templates

These are derived from the pattern in #4-A and proven structures from the creative ads guide.

**Template A — Direct Send (best for logo or design-first services):**
> "If you have [specific situation], send us your [asset] now. We will [specific benefit] before [deadline or consequence]."

**Template B — Keyword DM (best for volume tracking and warm audience):**
> "DM us [KEYWORD] and we will send you [specific thing] today."

**Template C — Risk Frame + Action (best for production and quality content):**
> "[Don't/Stop] [negative outcome]. [Action verb] your [specific thing] and we will [specific result]."

**Template D — Urgency + Specificity (best for seasonal or time-sensitive content):**
> "[Event/deadline] is [timeframe] away. [Action verb] your [specific thing] now so we can [specific outcome] before [consequence]."

## What to Test Next

1. **Keyword CTA test** — Add "DM us [word]" to one reel and track DM volume vs. profile activity
2. **Early CTA test** — Place a light CTA at second 8 (not just at the end) to capture partial-watch viewers
3. **Risk vs. benefit framing test** — Compare "don't gamble on consistency" vs. "get every piece looking identical" on similar topics
4. **Soft CTA on discovery reels, hard CTA on warm retargeting** — Test whether audience temperature changes CTA performance
