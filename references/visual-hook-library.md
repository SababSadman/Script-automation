---

## How to Use This File

A visual hook is what the viewer SEES in the first 1–3 seconds before they hear anything. It must stop the scroll on its own. Claude selects from this library based on the topic, brand style, and past performance data.

---

## Entry Format

```
### [Hook Name / Short Label]

**Description:**
[What is happening on screen? What does the camera show?]

**Best used for:**
[What topics, pain points, or emotional angles does this hook suit?]

**Notes:**
[Any performance data, tips, or context]
```

### Product Close-Up with Problem Context

**Description:**
Tight close-up shot of the product or service in action, immediately showing a result or a problem being solved. No intro, no branding — straight to the visual.

**Best used for:**
Product demos, before/after setups, service results, transformation hooks.

**Notes:**
Works well when the visual result is self-explanatory. Pairs with curiosity or result-based voice hooks.

---
### Person Reacting to a Problem

**Description:**
A real person (UGC-style) visibly frustrated, confused, or in pain — reacting to the exact problem the brand solves.

**Best used for:**
Pain point scripts, relatable problem topics, empathy-driven narratives.

**Notes:**
High relatability. Works best when the reaction is specific and exaggerated enough to be funny or deeply relatable.

---
### Before/After Split or Sequence

**Description:**
Opens with the "before" state — messy, broken, painful, or undesirable — then immediately cuts or transitions to the clean, fixed, desirable "after" state.

**Best used for:**
Transformation topics, results-driven scripts, service outcome showcases.

**Notes:**
Strong retention driver. Viewer wants to understand what caused the transformation.

---
### Text-on-Screen Statement with No Person

**Description:**
Clean background with a bold, single-line text statement that captures the pain point or bold claim. No face, no product — just the words.

**Best used for:**
Bold claim hooks, statistics, counterintuitive statements, curiosity hooks.

**Notes:**
Works well for thought leadership and educational content. Pairs with a voice hook that expands on the text.
