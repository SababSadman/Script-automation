## Instagram

**Best for:** Visual brands, lifestyle, B2B brand awareness, short-form video (Reels)
**Primary audience:** 18–44, visual-first consumers
**Posting frequency:** 1–2 feed posts per day, 3–10 Stories per day
**Best times:** 11am–1pm, 7–9pm

### Hook Rules
- **Hook window: First 1–3 seconds**
- First frame of Reels must hook visually — the opening image alone must stop the scroll before any sound is heard
- Reels get 2x the reach of static posts — always optimize the first frame
- High-quality visual in the first frame is non-negotiable; blurry or low-effort openings kill reach

### Format Constraints
- Reels: vertical 9:16
- Carousels: up to 10 slides; educational multi-slide content drives saves and shares
- Stories: use all interactive features (polls, questions, links) — they feed algorithm signals
- High-quality visuals required — low-quality images and video underperform significantly

### Tone
- More polished and intentional than TikTok
- Behind-the-scenes content works in Stories, not feed
- Content should feel curated but not over-produced
- Authentic brand voice preferred over corporate tone

### Algorithm Behavior
- Reels heavily prioritized over static posts in feed distribution
- **Saves and shares outweigh likes** — write CTAs that prompt saves ("save this") or shares ("send this to your team")
- Stories keep the brand at the top of followers' feeds — consistency matters more than perfection
- Using all features (polls, questions, Reels, carousels) signals to the algorithm that the account is active

### CTA Patterns for Instagram
- **Social Content:** "Save this if you've dealt with [pain]" / "Share this with whoever needs to see it" / "Follow for more on [topic]"
- **Ad Creative:** "DM us [keyword]" / "Click the link in bio" / "Book your free quote — link in bio"
- **Avoid:** Generic "like and subscribe" / "check us out" / vague "reach out" phrasing

### What to Avoid
- Low-quality images or video
- Too much text overlaid on images
- Ignoring Reels and Stories — static-only posting kills reach
- Only promotional content with no standalone value

## TikTok

**Best for:** Brand awareness, cold audience reach, viral potential, younger demographics
**Primary audience:** 16–34, entertainment-focused
**Posting frequency:** 1–4 posts per day
**Best times:** 7–9am, 12–3pm, 7–11pm

### Hook Rules
- **Hook window: First 1–2 seconds** — stricter than Instagram; scroll speed is significantly faster
- The first spoken word or visual must immediately create curiosity, recognition, or tension
- Native and unpolished hooks outperform highly produced intros
- No slow introductions — the point must land before second two

### Format Constraints
- Vertical only: 9:16
- **Keep under 30 seconds to start** — shorter content trains the algorithm before pushing longer videos
- Trending sounds can amplify reach — note sound direction in B-roll suggestions where relevant
- Repurposed horizontal video performs poorly — do not suggest cross-posting without vertical adaptation

### Tone
- Native, unpolished, and real — over-produced content is penalized by both the algorithm and the audience
- POV, day-in-the-life, and talking-to-camera formats outperform staged productions
- Educational content works when wrapped in entertainment — lead with the story, deliver the value inside it
- Hard sell language triggers the scroll; the ad must not feel like an ad

### Algorithm Behavior
- Watch completion rate is the primary distribution signal — drop-off kills reach
- Comment volume and reply-with-video patterns boost distribution
- Trending sounds and formats (stitches, duets) are rewarded
- Consistency of posting trains the algorithm to distribute the account's content more broadly

### CTA Patterns for TikTok
- **Social Content:** "Comment [word] and I'll send you [resource]" / "Follow for part 2" / "What would you do — drop it in the comments"
- **Ad Creative:** "DM us [keyword]" / "Click the link" — direct, no fluff; TikTok audiences reject soft-sell language
- **Avoid:** Hard sell ("order now", "buy today") on cold audiences — it signals ad and triggers scroll; overly corporate CTAs

### What to Avoid
- Overly produced or branded intros
- Ignoring trending sounds and formats
- Hard selling to cold audiences
- Repurposed horizontal video without adaptation
- Long hooks — anything that delays the point past 2 seconds

## Facebook

**Best for:** Local businesses, communities, older demographics, B2B decision-makers (35+), event promotion
**Primary audience:** 25–55+, community-oriented and relationship-driven
**Posting frequency:** 1–2 posts per day
**Best times:** 1–4pm weekdays

### Hook Rules
- **Hook window: First 3 seconds for video; first line of text for posts**
- Native video autoplay means the first visual frame must capture attention before sound
- Discussion-prompting questions in post text outperform pure statements
- Local and event-specific framing stops the scroll for community audiences

### Format Constraints
- Native video strongly preferred over external links — links to external sites are an algorithmic reach penalty
- Live video outperforms pre-recorded for organic reach
- Community and group content gets substantially higher organic reach than standard page posts
- Horizontal video acceptable; vertical also works in feed

### Tone
- Community-first and relationship-driven
- Local and event-based content resonates strongly — especially for Calgary-area businesses
- Conversational tone — content should spark replies and comments, not just consumption
- Hard sell language underperforms; value-first and story-first approaches work better

### Algorithm Behavior
- **Links to external sites significantly reduce organic reach** — keep users on platform
- Comments and shares are the primary amplification signals
- Groups reach is significantly higher than page reach — use groups for community content
- Responding to every comment signals active engagement and boosts distribution

### CTA Patterns for Facebook
- **Social Content:** "Drop a comment if this sounds familiar" / "Tag someone who needs to see this" / "What's your experience with [topic]?" (discussion prompt)
- **Ad Creative:** "Message us" / "Get your free quote" / "DM us [keyword]" — keep it on-platform where possible
- **Avoid:** "Click the link" as primary CTA on organic posts (reach penalty) / overly promotional language / ignoring comment sections after posting

### What to Avoid
- External links on organic posts (algorithmic reach killer)
- Pure promotional content with no community value
- Ignoring comments after posting
- Cross-posting from Instagram or TikTok without adapting text, aspect ratio, and tone
